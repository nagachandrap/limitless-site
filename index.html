What we do

Making everyday business operations smarter

Our principles

How we think about building

A few things we believe deeply about building technology for Indian businesses.

01

Meet people where they are

The best technology works within the tools people already use — not by asking them to change their behaviour or learn something new.

02

Accuracy above all

In business operations, wrong information is worse than no information. We are obsessive about reliability and never compromise on correctness.

03

Value before scale

We work deeply with a small number of customers to build something genuinely useful before expanding. Quality of impact matters more than quantity of users.
